## Agreed production model
- WORKING and normal GREEN share the same normal database.
- Dedicated UT TEST stays isolated through `ut_test_*`.
- Tag identity: unique 6-character `lower_number`; 078500 is not business data.
- Products: `bunner`, `hyller30`, `hyller60`.
- Warehouse lifecycle: `in_stock` -> `staged` -> `dispatched`.
- Compact availability counter: current `in_stock` minus still-unscanned quantities on active ramp orders; staged items are not subtracted twice.
