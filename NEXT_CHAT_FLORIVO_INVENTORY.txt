NEW ACCEPTED DESIGN — ZONES / WORKSHOPS:
Inventory session must be split into physical zones/rooms. Initial names:
- Varemottak
- Plukk
- Produksjon
- Kald
- Varm

Every scan/manual observation belongs to one zone.
Current zone stays active until changed.
PC reporting must work for whole warehouse and per-zone.
A zone can later be recounted/revised without silently overwriting other zones; preserve recount history.

Produksjon has many repeated configurations. Add a per-zone/current profile so operator can set e.g.:
1 Bunner + 3 Hyller
and every following RFID in that zone opens with Hyller=3 by default. Each RFID remains an individual record and duplicate protection remains active.

A hand-drawn warehouse map may later be supplied. Use it as basis for visual PC zone map; do not invent real geometry without evidence.

CANONICAL SAFETY:
