# FLORIVO BRAND / LOGO PROTOCOL

Protocol ID: `FLORIVO-BRAND-LOGO-2026-08-30`
Status: SELECTED CONCEPT / reference for future Florivo work

## Purpose
Preserve the selected Florivo visual concept so a new ChatGPT conversation can find it and continue work without redesigning from scratch.

## Origin
The visual concept started from a black T-shirt print photographed by the user: a very simple white line-art human figure with open/upraised arms reaching toward a small sun. The figure has no detailed face and feels cheerful, open and alive.

The concept was adapted for Florivo by surrounding the figure with flowers/plants and adding restrained colors.

## SELECTED VARIANT
The user selected the FIRST generated Florivo composition as the preferred reference (`перше напевно`).

Composition:
- black / very dark background;
- central original-style white line-art figure, arms open/up toward the sun;
- yellow/golden sun above/right of the figure;
- flowers and green foliage growing around both sides of the figure;
- colorful flowers (yellow/orange, blue, white, purple/pink accents), but keep the overall visual clean rather than photographic;
- white FLORIVO wordmark underneath;
- slogan under the name.

## Final slogan language
Use Norwegian:

`LEV • VOKS • BLOMSTRE`

Meaning:
- LEV = live
- VOKS = grow
- BLOMSTRE = bloom / flourish

This replaces the earlier Ukrainian draft `ЖИВИ • РОСТИ • СВІТИ` / `ЖИВИ • РОСТИ • ЦВІТИ`.

## App icon rule
For a small Android/app icon, DO NOT try to fit the full wordmark or slogan.
Create a simplified recognizable icon from:
- the white joyful figure;
- flowers / leaves;
- yellow sun;
- dark background.

The full `FLORIVO` + `LEV • VOKS • BLOMSTRE` composition is better for splash/start screen, presentation/visit-card style artwork, branding pages, etc.

## Important design intent
Do not replace the figure with a realistic person. Preserve the naive/simple hand-drawn line character that made the original T-shirt graphic memorable. Florivo adaptation should feel optimistic, botanical, friendly and recognizable.

## Next implementation
At the next Florivo app update:
1. derive production-ready app icon assets from this selected concept;
2. optionally add the full logo to splash/start screen;
3. keep existing app functionality/logic unchanged unless separately requested;
4. store final exported brand assets in the repository so later chats/builds can reuse the exact files.
